# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and technically constructive project.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, assume good faith while requiring
evidence, and respect privacy. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and
publishing private prompts, logs, credentials, or repository content are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Maintainers
may edit, remove, reject, or ban participation that violates these expectations.
