# Original cartoon sound pack

Generated for Stackhead by `python tools/audio/generate.py`. No recordings,
voices, songs or third-party meme clips were sampled. The project owner may use
and modify these original synthesized effects with the game.

Meme is a double duck honk; Bump/Glue are rubbery boings; Collapse is a descending
comic brass-style fail with a spring. Reward phrases, pickup, click, error, dash,
danger-wind and an original eight-second toy melody complete the pack.

The client routes funny sounds separately, limits voices, varies pitch, ducks
music and persists toggles. Before upload it uses pitch-shaped engine sounds.
**Local WAV files do not automatically play in a published Roblox experience.**

Import through Studio Asset Manager or Creator Dashboard, grant the experience
audio permission and copy IDs to `src/shared/Config/AudioAssets.luau`. Keys match
WAV filenames. Music starts only when its ID is configured. Check moderation and
listen inside Studio after import: https://create.roblox.com/docs/audio/assets.

Check stacked pickups, rare + meme pickups, banking and multiplayer crashes on
headphones and speakers. `manifest.json` records individual peaks and durations,
not a claim that the live mix has been audibly tested.
